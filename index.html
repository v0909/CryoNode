Why this works in Canada

AI infrastructure is constrained by power, cooling, carbon, and jurisdiction. Canada has structural advantages in all four.

Cold climate

Winnipeg, Regina, Saskatoon, and Edmonton offer long free-cooling seasons that reduce mechanical cooling needs.

Clean power

Hydroelectric-heavy grids improve the economics and ESG profile of AI compute.

Sovereign AI

Healthcare, government, universities, and finance need Canadian-hosted AI infrastructure.

Milestones

The POC is designed to de-risk the platform before scaling across cold Canadian cities.

Stage 1

Build CryoNode-1A, deploy at one Manitoba site, validate cooling, safety, monitoring, and remote control.

Stage 2

Run paid workloads with design partners. Measure GPU utilization, uptime, PUE, and heat recovery value.

Stage 3

Deploy 5–10 nodes across Manitoba and Saskatchewan. Prove fleet orchestration and billing.

Stage 4

Scale to 100+ nodes across Winnipeg, Regina, Saskatoon, Edmonton, Montreal, and Toronto suburbs.
